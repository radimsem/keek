# Idle Stretches are fast-forwarded, not cut

Cutting every span without an Action gives the cleanest video, but the Verification Video is evidence: a hard cut can drop an Action's delayed effect (an animation, a page load, a toast seconds later) and makes a 20 second hang look instant. The Verification Video keeps about 0.3 s before and 1.5 s after each Action, extends that while the screen is still changing, and shows the remaining Idle Stretches as Fast-forwards without labels. Their real length stays in the Session Timeline and the Raw Recording, kept beside the edit so any Verification Video can be checked against them. keek's own ffmpeg editor makes this edit until a release of Cua Driver's `recording render` can ([ADR 0009](0009-shared-pieces-go-to-cua-driver.md), [ADR 0013](0013-keek-times-the-session-on-stock-cua-driver.md)).

A Help Request's wait is the one span that is cut. While it is open the Verifier does nothing and the developer has not yet acted, so the wait shows nothing about the change under test, and a page with a blinking caret or an animation would otherwise play it at real speed for minutes. The wait is cut from the later of its start and the end of the last Action's window, with nothing in its place, so the screen the Verifier asked about stays in the video. Its real length stays on the Verdict Report. Each Takeover shows as about 2 s of its own footage, blurred, with "You were in control" centred on top.
